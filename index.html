How I work

A website usually passes through 3–4 people: copywriter, designer, front-end dev. Here, it's one. That's why it's faster and of higher quality.

01

Copywriting

Meaning first, visuals second. I unpack your brief and write copy that leads visitors to get in touch.

Timeline: 2–3 days Output: copy mapped to the page
02

Design

I design in Figma around the page's goal. Every decision is explained, never just "it looks nicer".

Timeline: 3–5 days Output: prototype
03

Build

Clean code, fast loading. It looks just as good on a laptop, tablet or phone.

Timeline: 3–6 days Output: responsive page
04

Animations

I use AI to create animations that used to require a separate developer.

Timeline: 1–4 days Output: smooth animation
05

CMS

I set up an admin panel so you can update copy, prices and cases yourself, with no designer and no fear of breaking anything.

Timeline: 1–2 days Output: admin panel + guide
06

Domain & hosting

I choose hosting that fits the project, upload the code and check the server. All accounts are in your name.

Timeline: 1 day Output: site live on your domain
07

Launch

Analytics run from day one, so you see where your clients come from right away.

Timeline: 1 day Output: PageSpeed 90+ report
08

Support

I stay in touch after launch: fixing bugs, making edits and improving the site as your business grows.

Timeline: from 1 month Output: a site that keeps growing

My path

Editing, graphics, interfaces. Each stage added a skill that now works for your site.

  1. 2022

    I learn Premiere Pro and video editing: timeline, rhythm, sound. That's where my habit of seeing a story through to the end comes from.

  2. 2023

    I take my first freelance projects and try my hand at motion design. I learn to understand what clients want and deliver on time.

  3. 2024

    I open Photoshop and dive headfirst into graphic design. My first Amazon product cards teach me the key thing: pretty isn't enough, it has to sell.

  4. 2025

    I open Figma and realize everything before was preparation. Editing taught me to hold attention, product cards taught me to sell, and web design brought it all together.

  5. 2026 Now

    I bring design and development together with Webflow, Figma and AI. I build sites myself, from mockup to launch.

  6. 2027 Next

    Leveling up my design with AI: complex animations, interactions and effects that used to be studio-only.
