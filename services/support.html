How it works

  • How to start

    Describe your task in two sentences. If you need an admin panel, tell me which copy, photos and prices you want to edit yourself.

  • Timeline

    I’ll send a quote within a day, and the work itself takes 1–3 days.

  • Payment

    On request: the price depends on the scope of the task. No subscription and no annual contract.

    I explain every decision: why it’s needed and how it affects the site. You have the final say.
